## Why SATE AI?

On-device AI inference is inherently susceptible to hardware resource exhaustion, temperature throttling, unexpected payload shapes, and quantization precision loss. The recipes in this cookbook provide copy-pasteable patterns and actionable architectures to build resilient Flutter AI experiences.
